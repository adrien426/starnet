# Rich warm room glow, without ceiling fixtures

The owner rejected the preceding e777e73a4 preview as dull and requested strong, vibrant warm lighting without visible ceiling lights. Source `011ba23a4` removes the overhead pendant painter, room wall-light bars and corridor lamp caps. Virtual light samples retain room-wide coverage. Light colour is now saturated gold (default 255,192,104), with related warm temperatures by room type; cool screens and viewport spill remain accents. Material colour gain is 0.8 and the shadow cool mix is reduced to 0.45. Steady glow increases from 0.06 to 0.13, prop spill from 0.6 to 0.9, and the existing low-resolution bloom runs at 0.25. CRT lab defaults match. Build glow clips to the same interior receiver as the station view.

The result was inspected in the actual :9197 app, furnished metal/plank rooms and the saved wood reference, plus Build mode. Ceiling hardware is absent. Light remains steady, room walls readable, and distribution broad. The proof also requires colour/luma to increase, so simply raising brightness cannot pass. These are baked material measurements, not whole-screen saturation claims. Evidence: `.worldshots/warm-glow/colour.json`, before/after material and furnished PNGs, coverage.log and settings.log.

The bloom probe executes the real production bloom function on native browser canvases, verifies a halo outside a bright patch and restored render state. In software-rendered headless Chrome, median time including a base-frame copy was 1.76ms at 960x640 and 5.24ms at 1920x1080. This is a microbenchmark, not installed-app FPS; hardware acceleration was disabled by the harness. The bloom uses 1/5-size buffers and refreshes them every other frame. There is no hardware ray tracing and no production per-frame pixel readback. Test driver and JSON: `.worldshots/warm-glow/bloom-proof.mjs`, `bloom.json`.
