# Raised wall corner lighting

The owner reported a dark, broken-looking upper-left wall section in the current preview. The wall artwork was intact; the interior-light receiver covered the floor footprint and straight north-wall faces, but omitted the lifted corner face outside those shapes. The lightmap therefore treated the exposed corner as exterior hull. Source `624e58ede` records the corner painter's actual clipped face spans and includes those spans in the receiver before subtracting crown pixels. Geometry, materials, warm-light settings and glow strength are unchanged. Both world and Build consume this shared receiver.

`dev/room-corner-light-proof.mjs` compares the current source against c0ca851e2 using actual browser canvases. It covers ribbed, plating and viewport walls; heights 0, 14, 30 and 50; and chamfer/round corners. All 24 cases have zero missing face coverage, zero unrelated receiver changes and zero base-art changes. The 30px chamfer had 694 missing face pixels before and zero after. Before/after renders, live screenshot and results.json are in `.worldshots/corner-light/`.

Registered `test/stationbake.chunk.test.js` now passes 90 assertions, including coverage of both raised top corners at three heights and two shapes, sky exclusion, repeated rebakes and chunk/full-lightmap parity. The shallow-wall test sample is inset below the crown: its original coordinate landed on the deliberately excluded crown at the right 14px chamfer. The native driver independently checks every recorded face pixel. Simulation-lighting passes 81 assertions. The actual :9197 preview was reloaded and the corner inspected in cinema mode. Build/live furnishing verification uses the shared native preview driver. Nearby overlapping walls are clipped by the same painter used to record these spans, but that specific geometry is not a separately registered regression.

Syntax and bug-register validation passed. The full standard `npm run test:fast` passed all 723 steps, exit 0; log: `.worldshots/corner-light/test-fast.log`. The furnished station/Build proof also passed with no degraded layers and unchanged station data. Source fingerprints were refreshed in `26ec96fb4`; later commits update QA records only.
